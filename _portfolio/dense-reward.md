title: "Dense Dynamics-Aware Reward Synthesis"
excerpt: "Reward shaping from prior experience and expert demonstrations"
collection: portfolio
---

Many continuous control problems can be formulated as sparse-reward reinforcement learning tasks. Discovering sequences of actions that lead to non-zero reward becomes exponentially harder as the task horizon increases.

This project introduces a systematic reward-shaping framework that distills information from a task-agnostic prior dataset and a small number of task-specific expert demonstrations to synthesize dense dynamics-aware rewards, substantially accelerating learning.

**Related publication:** [L4DC](/publication/2025-koprulu-dense-reward) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01114)
