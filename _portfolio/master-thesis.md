Service mobile robots often navigate in human-rich environments where pedestrians are highly dynamic. This master's thesis proposes:

* **MPC-GPred**: an inverse dynamic game method based on model predictive control to model pedestrians' individual and group behavior, with LQ approximation for analytic Nash trajectory prediction.
* **MPC-GNav**: a dynamic game navigation method that includes the robot as an agent in the game, with feedback linearization and layered planning for large scenarios.

Methods were compared with RVO and CADRL in simulation, and MPC-GNav was implemented on the "Jiao Long" smart wheelchair in pedestrian-rich corridors and halls.

**Year:** 2023 · Shanghai Jiao Tong University
