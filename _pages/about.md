Tianyu Qiu is a Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu/). He is a research assistant in the [Control and Learning for Autonomous Robotics (CLeAR) Lab](https://clearoboticslab.github.io/), advised by Prof. [David Fridovich-Keil](https://dfridovi.github.io/). Prior to UT Austin, Tianyu received his M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2023 and 2020, respectively. His research interests include reinforcement learning, game theory, social navigation, and multi-robot planning.
