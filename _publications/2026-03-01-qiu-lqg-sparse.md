Linear-quadratic Gaussian games provide a framework for modeling strategic interactions in multi-agent systems, where agents must estimate system states from noisy observations while also making decisions to optimize a quadratic cost. This paper considers LQG games with sparse interagent observations and designs a distributed estimator that balances estimation effectiveness with interagent measurement sparsity via a group lasso problem, while agents implement feedback Nash strategies based on their state estimates.
