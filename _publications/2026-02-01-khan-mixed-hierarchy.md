Multi-robot coordination often exhibits hierarchical structure, with some robots' decisions depending on the planned behaviors of others. We study forest-structured mixed-hierarchy games that combine simultaneous (Nash) and hierarchical (Stackelberg) decision-making, introduce a quasi-policy approximation that removes higher-order policy derivatives, and develop an inexact Newton method for efficiently solving the resulting approximated KKT systems.
